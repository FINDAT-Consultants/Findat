# FINDAT desktop auto-arrangement

- Newly dropped or uploaded desktop documents use the next free grid position.
- Documents are aligned vertically and continue into compact adjacent columns.
- Existing overlapping desktop positions are corrected automatically on load.
- FINDAT Cloud, Applications, and Trash remain in a protected left-hand column.
- Manually moved icons snap to the nearest unoccupied cell instead of covering another icon.
- Large icon collections continue into a scrollable overflow area rather than stacking.
- Supabase-only storage and green saved ticks remain unchanged.
